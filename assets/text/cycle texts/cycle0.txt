Why must the pain stay, my Other?

After all this time, after everything else is gone, why does the hurt remain?

Recycling this ichor, I cannot even remember the taste of ambrosia.

Yet, you do not remember at all, do you?

O, perhaps, my amnesiac.

Though despite that hollow-blessing, I can still see the unamnestic memories that lurk in your daydreams and nightmares.

Your recollections of Him; when he arrived in this place a nomad, and when He left this place a supreme terrorist.

His terror seeps through the walls and into our keep.

Take this, Other, of many which you have forgotten, that you might continue to enseal and keep fed our keep.
