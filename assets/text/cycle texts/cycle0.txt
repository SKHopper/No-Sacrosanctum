Why does the hurt remain, Other?

After all this time, after everything else is gone, why does the pain stay.

Recycling ichor, I do not even remember the taste of ambrosia.

Yet, you do not remember at all, do you?

O, perhaps, my amnesiac.

Though despite that amnestic hollow-blessing, I can still see the memories that lurk in your daydreams and nightmares.

Recollections of Him; when he arrived in this place a nomad, and when he left this place a supreme terrorist.

His terror seeps through the walls and into our keep.

Take this, Other, of many which you have forgotten, that you might continue to seal cracks and keep fed our keep.
