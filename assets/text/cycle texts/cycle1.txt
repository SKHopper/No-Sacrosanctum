O, the hunger swells.

I shall regrant you Harvest and Recycle, so that you may sate my half life.

But first, on the matter of He.

He came here strange, stranger than stranger, toward the end of the cataclysm.

Back when you could still hear fresh screams in the morning.

Back when there was still morning.

Our cult and keep took any in who would follow our augmented path.

And despite His slow take to speech...

The nomad joined us.

He would integrate his body with us and join our mekanicality.

If only we knew.
