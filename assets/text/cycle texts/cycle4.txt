I have burdened you with reminding of our doom's fear.

Now recometh His wrath.

This was not sighted, it was not wise or prophesizing.

It was mindless.

Awesome.

After a millennium of augmentation in our walls in our keep in our world of fire.

Some nerve or servo in this one snapped.

His wires bulged vascular; his veins burned with battery acid.

He was our most powerfilled, and his distaste for the nightmare and warfare around us was like bile.
