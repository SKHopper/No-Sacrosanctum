So again, here He was, our convert coven cultist at the end of the world.

Growing familiar with our kin and ken and keep. Knower of things yet unknown.

Genius. Growing technological.

Yet within Him I saw something else. Two souls: fear, and wrath, opposed.

I fret to relieve you, Other, of that amnestic empty-blessed forgetting.

But you must know His fear.

I suppose it was always and ever present within His protheticism.

For all the up coming He saw, there was a spiralling daunter.

A horror you could almost see in the corner of His eye.

Something inevitable.

The madness His fear brought the once-nomad exuded.
